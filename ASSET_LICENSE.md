## Covered paths

Paths are relative to the repository root.

- `client/public/logo-dark.svg`
- `client/public/favicon.ico`

- `docs/src/public/logo-app-icon.svg`

Icon-drawing source under `assets/src/` is MIT-licensed; the shipped files listed below and their visual design are not. Other files under `client/public/` (and elsewhere) are not listed here; follow their own licenses and any attribution already shown in the product.
